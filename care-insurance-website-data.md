### Claim process (same flow for most products)

**Cashless**
1. Planned hospitalisation: intimate Care at least 48 hours prior. Emergency: within 24 hours of admission.
2. Go to a network hospital and visit the TPA/insurance desk.
3. Submit the pre-authorisation request. Care accepts or rejects.
4. Get treated and discharged. Care settles directly with the hospital.

**Reimbursement**
1. Intimate (same 48 h / 24 h rule).
2. Get treated at any hospital, pay the bill, keep **original** receipts.
3. Submit the claim form with documents after discharge.
4. Care verifies and pays into the bank account.

### Care Supreme FAQs (usable verbatim as FAQ content)
- Can I add children? Yes, up to 2 children and 2 adults on a floater.
- Medical tests before buying? Possibly, depending on health status.
- Annual health check-up? Yes, as an optional benefit with the base policy.
- What if SI is exhausted? Unlimited automatic recharge restores it each time within the policy year.

### Fine print shown on the page
"Starting at ₹13/day" is calculated for an individual aged 18, ₹7 lakh SI, Zone 3 city, with Room Rent Modification and Sublimit add-ons. Tax benefit is subject to current tax law. Read the brochure and policy wording before buying.

### 5.2 Important limitation
**Care's actual premium rates are not published on any page I could read.** The quote tool at abacus.careinsurance.com is a dynamic app, and rate tables sit in the filed prospectus and the partner (Faveo) portal. I cannot give you accurate rate numbers, and guessing them would mislead your customers.

### 5.3 Three ways to build the calculator

| Option | How | Pros / cons |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Redirect/embed (recommended to start)** | Your form collects members, ages, mobile, then opens the Abacus quote link | Always accurate; lead capture on your side |
| **B. Own rate-table calculator** | Download the rate card from your Care agent/partner login and load it as JSON | Fully branded; you must update it on every premium revision |
| **C. Partner API** | Ask your Care relationship manager for API access | Best experience; requires approval |

### 5.4 Calculator logic for option B (structure only)

```
for each insured member:
    base = RATE[plan][sumInsured][zone][ageBand(member.age)]       // from rate card
    if coverType == "individual": premium += base
if coverType == "floater":
    premium = RATE_FLOATER[plan][sumInsured][zone][eldestAgeBand][2A2C-combination]
premium += addOnPremiums (room rent modification, OPD, Protect Plus, ...)
premium *= tenureFactor            // 2-yr / 3-yr long-term discount per rate card
premium -= loyalty/renewal discounts (up to 30% renewal discount on Care Supreme)
gst = premium * GST_RATE           // confirm current GST on individual health policies
total = premium + gst
```

Validation rules to apply in the form:
- Adult 18–99, child 90 days – 24 years
- Floater max 2 adults + 2 children; individual max 6 persons
- At least one adult must be on the policy if a child is added
- Mobile: 10 digits, starts 6–9 (for +91)
- Show "indicative premium – final premium after underwriting"

## 7. Excluded / "blacklist" hospitals

- Official wording on the page: expenses at a hospital, practitioner or provider specifically excluded by the insurer and disclosed on its website or notified to policyholders are **not admissible**. In life-threatening situations or after an accident, expenses **up to the stage of stabilisation** are payable, but not the complete claim.
- Official downloadable list: `.../download_center/Excluded_List.pdf`
- Recommendation: label this "Excluded / Non-preferred Hospitals" on your public site rather than "Blacklist". It matches Care's own wording and avoids defaming hospitals.

## 10. Compliance checklist (important for an insurance website)

- Show: "Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation", IRDAI registration no. 148 for Care, and the product UIN on each plan page.
- If you are an agent/POSP/broker, show **your own** licence number, validity and the insurer(s) you represent. Do not present the site as Care Health Insurance itself.
- Add the IRDAI fraud warning: IRDAI does not sell policies, announce bonuses or invest premiums.
- Add the premium-calculation disclaimer: "indicative; subject to underwriting; read the brochure and wording."
- Collect explicit consent for calls/WhatsApp, include a privacy policy, and respect DND (Care links its "Do not call" page).
- Do not copy Care's logos, images or customer reviews without permission. Write your own copy from the facts above.
- Re-check benefits and premiums against the latest brochure on every product revision.
